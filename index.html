Cyber IQ challenge

Quick practice to reinforce safer online habits.

Quick safety checklist

Keep these habits in mind every day.

🔑 Keep secrets secret

Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords, even with someone claiming to be support staff.

🕰️ Slow down

Scammers often use urgency, fear or excitement to pressure you into acting.

🌐 Verify independently

Use an official app, website or number—not the link or number in a suspicious message.

India cybercrime support

If you suspect a scam or financial fraud, act promptly.

Financial cyber fraud helpline: 1930

Report cybercrime through the official portal: cybercrime.gov.in. Contact your bank/payment provider immediately if money has been lost. Keep evidence such as screenshots and transaction details.

CyberSakhi is an educational prototype, not a government service or certified fraud detector. Risk labels are indicative, not definitive.
